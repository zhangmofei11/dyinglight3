Boss Guides

Defeat every boss with proven strategies.

🧟

The Brute

Act 1 - Mutated survivor boss

Easy

Attack Patterns

  • • Ground Slam: AoE damage, dodge away
  • • Charge: Running attack, dodge sideways
  • • Throw: Picks up debris, throw back

Strategy

  • • Stay at medium range
  • • Attack after his slam
  • • Use environment for cover
👤

Raven

Act 2 - Antagonist lieutenant

Medium

Attack Patterns

  • • Blade Combo: Three-hit melee attack
  • • Gun Fire: Pistol shots, take cover
  • • Grapple: Tackle attack, dodge

Strategy

  • • Close distance quickly
  • • Block and counter his attacks
  • • Use grenades to stagger
🦇

The Screamer Queen

Act 3 - Volatile-infected boss

Medium

Attack Patterns

  • • Sonic Scream: Area stun, run away
  • • Swarm Call: Summons volatile minions
  • • Pounce: Long-range leap attack

Strategy

  • • Kill minions first
  • • Use ranged weapons
  • • Avoid the scream attack
💀

The GRE Director

Act 4 - Final antagonist

Hard

Attack Patterns

  • • Minigun: Sustained fire, take cover
  • • Missile Strike: Tracking missiles, dodge
  • • Exosuit Punch: Heavy melee, block

Strategy

  • • Use cover strategically
  • • Shoot weak points on exosuit
  • • Save Beast Mode for final phase
👹

The Beast (Final)

Act 5 - The truth behind everything

Extreme

Attack Patterns

  • • Claw Swipes: Fast melee, dodge roll
  • • Ground Pound: AoE shockwave
  • • Beast Rush: Charging attack
  • • Enrage: Faster attacks at low health

Strategy

  • • Use Beast Mode to match his power
  • • Target the glowing weak points
  • • Keep moving, never stop
