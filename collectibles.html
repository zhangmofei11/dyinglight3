Collectible Types

Supply Drops

Blue glowing containers scattered throughout the map.

  • • Contain weapons, mods, and materials
  • • Often guarded by enemies
  • • Use Survivor Sense to locate

GRE Logs

Audio logs and documents revealing the story.

  • • Unlocks lore about the outbreak
  • • Required for "Historian" achievement
  • • Found in story-critical locations

Mods

Attachments that enhance weapons.

  • • Damage boosters
  • • Handling improvements
  • • Elemental effects

Safe Houses

Unlock new safe houses for fast travel.

  • • Restore health
  • • Save progress
  • • Access crafting
