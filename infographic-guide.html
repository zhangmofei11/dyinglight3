INFECTED TYPES

Know your enemies

🧟 REGULAR INFECTED

😵

Walker

Basic infected. Slow, predictable.

Kill: Any weapon

🏃

Runner

Fast but fragile.

Kill: Light weapons

💪

Renegade

Armored. Takes more hits.

Kill: Heavy weapons

👹 SPECIAL INFECTED

🔥 Screamer

  • • Alerts nearby infected
  • • Weak to silenced weapons
  • • Kill quickly to avoid horde
  • • Weak point: Head

💀 Demolisher

  • • Massive health pool
  • • Ground slam attack
  • • Use environment for advantage
  • • Weak point: Backpack

🧱 Charger

  • • Charging attack
  • • Can one-shot kill
  • • Dodge sideways to avoid
  • • Weak point: Head

☠️ Toxic

  • • Emits poison gas
  • • Avoid melee range
  • • Use ranged weapons
  • • Weak point: Gas tank

🌙 NIGHT HORRORS

Volatile

Elite infected. Extremely dangerous at night. Avoid unless necessary.

Night Hunter

Fast predator. Hunts players specifically. Use UV light to stun.

Bloater

Explosive infected. Creates toxic cloud. Long range weapons preferred.

🎯 WEAKNESSES SUMMARY

Fire

  • • Effective vs most infected
  • • Quick crowd control
  • • Uses firebombs or molotovs

Electricity

  • • Stuns special infected
  • • Good for takedowns
  • • Use shock arrows

⚔️ COMBAT STRATEGY

1

Identify

Spot special infected early

2

Kill screamers first

Use right weapon for type
