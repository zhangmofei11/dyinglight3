Skills Guide

Master all skill trees and build your perfect survivor.

Skill Trees

Parkour

Movement and traversal abilities.

  • • Quick Ladder - Climb faster
  • • Safe Landing - Reduced fall damage
  • • Wall Run - Run along walls
  • • Grapple Hook - Swing across gaps
  • • Free Running - Faster parkour movement

Best for: Exploration, evasion, traversal

Combat

Fighting and melee abilities.

  • • Power Strikes - Heavier attack damage
  • • Critical Strike - Chance for 2x damage
  • • Combat Reset - Reset combos on kill
  • • Shield Master - Block and counter
  • • Beast Mode Boost - Extended Beast duration

Best for: Direct confrontation, boss fights

Survival

Health, crafting, and resources.

  • • Ninja - Quieter movement
  • • Medic - Faster healing
  • • Lucky - Better loot drops
  • • Craftsman - Reduced material costs
  • • Night Vision - See in darkness

Best for: Night gameplay, resource management

Recommended Builds

Parkour Master Build

Maximize mobility and evasion.

  • • Parkour: 15 points (max)
  • • Survival: 8 points
  • • Combat: 5 points

Playstyle: Run past enemies, complete missions stealthily

Combat God Build

Destroy everything in your path.

  • • Combat: 15 points (max)
  • • Survival: 10 points
  • • Parkour: 3 points

Playstyle: Fight every horde, use Beast Mode often

Night Runner Build

Perfect for nighttime missions.

  • • Survival: 12 points
  • • Parkour: 10 points
  • • Combat: 6 points

Playstyle: Night missions, loot hunting, stealth

Beast Master Build

Optimized for Beast Mode damage.

  • • Combat: 12 points
  • • Beast: 10 points
  • • Survival: 6 points

Playstyle: Save Beast Mode for tough encounters

Beast Mode Abilities

Beast Mode is unlocked in Act 4. While active, you become invincible and deal massive damage with claw attacks.

Duration
30 seconds base
Cooldown
5 minutes
Damage
200% melee
Health Regen
Full while active
