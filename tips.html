Tips & Tricks

Essential advice for surviving the apocalypse.

Combat Tips

🎯 Master the Counter

Block just before an enemy attacks to counter. Counters deal bonus damage and stun enemies.

🔄 Keep Moving

Never stand still. Circle around enemies and attack from behind for critical hits.

💪 Use Your Environment

Explosive barrels, car alarms, and ziplines can help crowd control or escape.

🗡️ Weapon Durability

Keep multiple weapons rotated. Don't let your best weapon break in a fight.

Survival Tips

❤️ Healing Priority

Bandages heal over time. Use medkits only in emergencies. Always carry backup.

🎒 Inventory Management

Drop low-value items to make room. Prioritize weapon mods and crafting materials.

🏠 Safe Houses

Unlock safe houses early. They provide fast travel, saves, and crafting stations.

📦 Loot Smart

Supply drops are valuable but often dangerous. Use stealth or distractions.

Night Gameplay

  • • Volatiles are deadly - Avoid at all costs until you're powerful
  • • Use UV Flashlights - Volatiles flee from UV light
  • • Night = Better loot - Enemies drop more valuable items at night
  • • Flares are crucial - Use them to create safe zones
  • • Parkour saves lives - Get to high ground to escape danger

General Tips

Start Slow

Don't rush combat early. Focus on parkour and exploration first.

Complete Side Quests

They unlock safe houses and give valuable rewards.

Save Often

Manual save at safe houses. Death means losing progress.
