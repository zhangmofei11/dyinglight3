VEHICLE GUIDE

Drive to survive

🚗 CARS

Sedan

  • • Balanced speed and handling
  • • 4 passenger capacity
  • • Good armor potential
  • • Common find early game

Sports Car

  • • Highest top speed
  • • Poor armor
  • • 2 passenger capacity
  • • Best for quick escapes

🚙 SUVs & TRUCKS

SUV

  • • Best durability
  • • Ram through hordes
  • • 4-6 passenger capacity
  • • Slower acceleration

Pickup Truck

  • • Good off-road performance
  • • Open bed for cargo
  • • 2-4 passenger capacity
  • • Moderate armor

🏍️ MOTORCYCLES

Bike Types

  • • Sport bikes - Fastest but fragile
  • • Choppers - Balanced performance
  • • Dirt bikes - Best off-road capability
  • • Single rider only - No passengers

🔧 VEHICLE MODS

🛡️

Armor Plating

Damage reduction. Weight penalty.

⚡

Engine Upgrade

Speed boost. Fuel consumption up.

💡

Ram Bar

Better zombie kills. Front damage.

🏎️ DRIVING TIPS

Survival

  • • Keep gas tank above 25%
  • • Use headlights at night
  • • Avoid dense infected areas
  • • Check mirrors for followers

Combat

  • • Drive slow for passenger aiming
  • • Ram at 30-40 mph for kills
  • • Use horn to draw infected
  • • Exit vehicle toward safe area
