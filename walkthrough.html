Main Story Missions

Act 1

Awakening

Kyle Crane wakes up in the Castor Woods with no memory. Learn basic survival and parkour.

Key Objectives

  • • Find the survivor camp
  • • Learn basic combat
  • • Escape the initial horde

Unlocks

  • • Basic parkour moves
  • • Simple weapons
  • • Safe house access
Act 2

The New Order

Join a faction and establish your base. Make choices that affect the story.

Key Objectives

  • • Choose your faction
  • • Complete faction missions
  • • Meet key NPCs

Unlocks

  • • Advanced parkour
  • • Crafting recipes
  • • Faction-specific gear
Act 3

The GRE

Uncover the truth about GRE and their experiments. Discover your connection to the outbreak.

Key Objectives

  • • Infiltrate GRE facilities
  • • Collect evidence
  • • Survive the chase sequences

Unlocks

  • • Grappling hook
  • • Vehicle access
  • • Beast Mode teaser
Act 4

Beast Unleashed

Unlock Beast Mode and use its power against the growing threat.

Key Objectives

  • • Complete Beast trials
  • • Master Beast abilities
  • • Confront the main antagonist

Unlocks

  • • Full Beast Mode
  • • Legendary weapons
  • • True ending path
Act 5

Requiem

The final confrontation. Choose between two endings based on your actions.

Key Objectives

  • • Final assault on enemy base
  • • Make the critical choice
  • • See your ending

Endings

  • • Good: Save the survivors
  • • Bad: Personal vendetta
